Boundary builder

Internal tool, not linked from the guide. Pick an area on the left, click its corners on the map, close the shape, add your MLS numbers. Everything saves as you go. When you are done, hit Export and send me what is in the box.

Zones (the seven Pocatello and Chubbuck areas): click each corner, then Close shape. Six to ten corners is plenty — rough is fine, it just needs to be right.

Towns (Idaho Falls and friends): one click drops a pin. Click again to move it.
